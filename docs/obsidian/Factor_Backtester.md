# Factor Backtester

## Overview
The **Factor Model Backtester** (`app/quant/factor_backtest.py`) converts static cross-sectional factor rankings into a realistic long/short portfolio, generating a daily return stream. 

## Architecture & Implementation
The backtester evaluates the alpha signals generated by the [[Factor_Library]] by simulating a monthly rebalanced portfolio.

### Key Components:
- **Strategy Design**:
  - Ranks all universe stocks by composite alpha.
  - Goes long the top quintile (20%) and short the bottom quintile (20%).
  - Equal-weights within each leg to maintain dollar neutrality.
- **Transaction Costs Model**: 
  - Explicitly accounts for typical Indian retail costs on rebalance days.
  - Implements round-trip brokerage (e.g., 5 bps) and estimated market impact (e.g., 10 bps).
- **Execution Flow**: 
  - Takes `price_data` and fundamental dictionaries.
  - Walks forward over defined `rebalance_days` (default 21).
  - Outputs a `FactorBacktestResult` containing detailed metrics (Sharpe, Drawdown, Turnover) and the simulated daily returns.

## Role in the Pipeline
The `daily_returns` series output by this backtester is fed directly into the [[Multi_Strategy_Engine]], representing the "factor investing" strategy sleeve.

## Constraints
- **AGENTS.md Rule 1**: Deterministic historical simulation. No LLM involvement in backtest execution.
