# Paper books restatement — 27 Nov 2026

**What happened.** All four paper books were archived and restarted together at **15:40 IST on 27-Nov-2026**, after the session close. This opened **epoch 2**. Nothing was deleted: epoch 1 is kept unchanged.

## Why

The restart reflects an engine version change. Three things changed together:

1. **Stale-signal bug.**
   - The server had been down on 26-Nov.
   - At 10:10 on 27-Nov the daily engine traded on cached signals from the 25-Nov close. It opened KOTAKBANK/PNB in the validated book.
   - At 10:12, after the fix, it closed the position on 26-Nov signals.
   - The validated book's epoch-1 loss is almost entirely the cost of that two-minute round trip.
   - **Fix:** fills now require signals from the last completed session, and the engine recomputes them from completed bars if they're missing (`app/quant/paper_book.py`: `signal_status`, `_ensure_last_close`). If neither works, the engine doesn't trade.
   - **Regression tests:** `tests/test_paper_book.py::test_stale_signals_trigger_last_close_recompute` and `::test_stale_signals_never_trade_when_recompute_fails`.
2. **Cost model updated** to the verified NSE/Zerodha schedule, including the 1-Apr-2026 F&O STT change (`app/quant/costs.py`).
3. **Shadow admission rule adopted** for intraday experiments. A strategy is forward-tested only if its training-window gross edge is > 0. This rule was adopted *after* the v1 out-of-sample results were known, and that is disclosed in the report.

All four books restarted together so that only an engine change triggers a restart. Losing books are never selectively reset.
